# 💎 Luxury Retail Store Feasibility Study Tool

## Comprehensive Financial Analysis for Watches & Jewelry Retail Business

A professional-grade financial feasibility analysis tool designed specifically for luxury retail store planning in the watches and jewelry sector. Built with Streamlit for easy deployment and zero-code operation.

## 🎯 Key Features

### ✅ Complete with ALL Specified Requirements:

#### **Section 1: Store Setup Inputs (Granular Level)**
- **Location & Space Parameters:**
  - Store location (city, mall/street, zone classification)
  - Retail and storage area (sq ft/sq m)
  - Base rent per sq ft/month
  - Fit-out cost per sq ft
  - Security deposit (months of rent)
  - Initial renovation/branding costs

- **Product Mix & Inventory:**
  - Product categories (watches, jewelry, accessories with % mix)
  - Average unit retail price per category
  - Initial inventory investment per category
  - Supplier payment terms (days)
  - Landed cost % (duties, shipping, insurance)
  - Inventory turnover target (days)
  - Shrinkage/loss allowance (%)

- **Operational Timeline:**
  - Lease signing to opening (months)
  - Pre-opening expenses period
  - Ramp-up period to stabilization (months)

#### **Section 2: Financial Assumptions**
- **Revenue Assumptions:**
  - Daily foot traffic projections (year 1, 2, 3+)
  - Conversion rate (% of visitors to buyers)
  - Average transaction value by category
  - Seasonal variation factors (by quarter)
  - Year-over-year growth rates
  - Peak vs. non-peak day revenue ratios
  - Payment method mix (cash/card/installment %)

- **Cost Assumptions:**
  - Gross margin by product category (%)
  - Staff costs (detailed by position with headcount)
  - Marketing spend (% of revenue + fixed launch cost)
  - Utilities cost per sq ft
  - Annual rent escalation (%)
  - Credit card processing fees (%)
  - Loyalty program costs (% of sales)

- **Working Capital Assumptions:**
  - Receivables days (if B2B component exists)
  - Payables days to suppliers
  - Minimum cash balance required
  - Forex hedging costs (if importing)

- **Financing Assumptions:**
  - Equity contribution amount
  - Debt amount and interest rate
  - Loan tenure and moratorium period
  - Required IRR/hurdle rate
  - Discount rate for NPV calculation

#### **Section 3: Detailed P&L (5-Year Monthly/Annual)**
- Revenue by product category
- Cost of goods sold by category (with landed costs and shrinkage)
- Gross profit and margin %
- **Complete Operating Expenses Breakdown:**
  - Salaries (by position with headcount)
  - Rent and common area maintenance
  - Utilities (electricity, AC, water)
  - Marketing and promotions
  - Insurance
  - Repairs and maintenance
  - Professional fees
  - Technology/POS subscriptions
  - Security services
  - Consumables and supplies
  - Payment processing fees
  - Loyalty program costs
- EBITDA and EBITDA margin
- Depreciation and amortization
- EBIT and EBIT margin
- Interest expense
- Tax expense (UAE corporate tax 9%)
- Net profit and margin %
- Cash flow from operations
- Principal repayments
- Free cash flow
- Cumulative cash flow
- Break-even analysis

#### **Section 4: High-Level Executive Summary**
- **Key Performance Indicators:**
  - Total investment required
  - Payback period (months)
  - NPV at assumed discount rate
  - IRR (%)
  - Break-even month
  - Year 1-5 revenue and profit summary
  - Average monthly revenue at stabilization
  - Peak funding requirement
  - Return on investment (%)
  - Debt service coverage ratio (if applicable)
  - Sensitivity analysis (what-if scenarios)

- **Visual Indicators:**
  - ✅ **Traffic light system for feasibility** (🟢 GREEN / 🟡 AMBER / 🔴 RED based on IRR vs. hurdle rate)
  - **Monthly cash flow waterfall** chart
  - **Revenue build-up trajectory** chart
  - **Margin trend analysis** chart
  - Cumulative cash flow visualization with break-even indicator

## 📊 Financial Metrics Calculated

### Investment Returns:
- **NPV (Net Present Value)** - at user-defined discount rate
- **IRR (Internal Rate of Return)** - annual %
- **ROI (Return on Investment)** - %
- **Payback Period** - months
- **Break-even Point** - month when cumulative cash flow turns positive

### Profitability:
- **Gross Profit & Margin** - by category and overall
- **EBITDA & EBITDA Margin** - earnings before interest, tax, depreciation, amortization
- **EBIT & EBIT Margin** - operating profit
- **Net Profit & Net Margin** - after all expenses and taxes

### Cash Flow:
- **Operating Cash Flow** - from business operations
- **Free Cash Flow** - after debt repayments
- **Cumulative Cash Flow** - running total from inception

### Liquidity:
- **Peak Funding Requirement** - maximum negative cash position
- **Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)** - ability to service debt
